## 2026-04-18 — Contoso opens Wrocław manufacturing site Q3 2026

**Source:** Contoso Industries press release, 2026-04-18

Contoso Industries AG announced today that its new specialty-chemicals
manufacturing facility in Wrocław, Poland will begin commissioning in Q3 2026.
The site, an investment of EUR 180 million, is part of Contoso's stated
nearshoring strategy and will produce polymer additives previously imported
from APAC.

> *"This site is a cornerstone of our European supply resilience plan,"*
> said CEO Dr. Markus Vogel. *"It will reduce our lead times to European
> customers by 30 % and meaningfully de-risk our intra-EU dependencies."*

Initial annual capacity: 24,000 t. Workforce: ~280 FTE at full ramp (2027).

**K+N implication:** likely shift of ~120 TEU/year out of APAC Sea inbound;
opens Contract Logistics conversation for finished-goods warehousing.

## 2026-04-02 — Contoso CFO interview: supply-chain resilience and dual-sourcing

**Source:** Chemical Industry Weekly, interview with CFO Eva Lindqvist, 2026-04-02

In a long-form interview, Contoso CFO **Eva Lindqvist** outlined the
company's post-2024 supply-chain strategy: aggressive dual-sourcing for
critical intermediates, ESG-credentialed logistics partners, and end-to-end
shipment visibility as a board-level KPI.

Selected quotes:

> *"We're done with single-source dependencies. Every critical molecule has
> two qualified suppliers and two qualified logistics routes by end of 2026."*

> *"Our 3PL partners need to deliver shipment-level Scope 3 data. Not just
> annual averages — actual per-shipment CO₂e. That's table stakes for us now."*

> *"We're willing to pay a premium for resilience. We're not willing to pay
> a premium for marketing claims."*

**K+N implication:** opportunity to lead with shipment-level CO₂e reporting
and Book-and-Claim sustainable fuel certificates. Anna Berg's team will be
held accountable to these KPIs.

## 2026-03-11 — Contoso publishes ESG roadmap update

**Source:** Contoso Industries Sustainability Report 2025 update, 2026-03-11

Key updates to Contoso's published ESG roadmap:

- **Scope 1+2 net-zero target:** confirmed for **2040**
- **Scope 3 vendor data push:** vendor reporting requirement starts **H2 2026**
- **Logistics partners:** required to report shipment-level CO₂e by Q4 2026
- **Sustainable Aviation Fuel (SAF):** all air freight to be ≥ 10 % SAF-blended
  by 2027
- **Book-and-Claim:** accepted for SAF and biofuel road freight, **must be
  certified by ISCC or RSB**

**K+N implication:** the Pharma cold-chain proposal due to Anna Berg by
2026-06-30 must include a CO₂e reporting commitment and a Book-and-Claim
SAF option.

## 2026-02-24 — Contoso wins 3-year supply contract with European pharma major

**Source:** Pharma Logistics Today, 2026-02-24

Contoso Industries has been awarded a **3-year exclusive supply contract**
with an unnamed top-5 European pharma company for niche intermediates used
in oncology APIs. Contract value: estimated EUR 220 million over 3 years.

Initial volume: 18 t/month of temperature-controlled product, flowing from
Contoso Hamburg and Frankfurt sites to the customer's European fill-and-finish
network.

**K+N implication:** ~40 % uplift in Contoso Pharma cold-chain Road Logistics
volume from Q3 2026 onward. Frankfurt cross-dock capacity must be validated —
particularly relevant given the open temperature-excursion case (CASE-2026-0157).
