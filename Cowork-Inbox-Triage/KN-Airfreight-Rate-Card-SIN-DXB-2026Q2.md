| > 5,000 kg | quote on request | quote on request |

All-in rate **includes**:
- Pickup Singapore (radius 50 km from Changi, business hours)
- Origin handling & x-ray screening (RA3 / KC compliant)
- Master and house AWB issuance
- Singapore export customs clearance (standard HS codes, no DGR)
- Airfreight main leg (capacity reserved on weekly allotment)
- DXB import customs clearance (standard goods, DAP)
- Last-mile delivery to JAFZA or DAFZA (radius 35 km from DXB)
- Standard liability per Warsaw / Montreal Convention

All-in rate **excludes**:
- Fuel surcharge (FSC) - see §3
- Security surcharge (SSC) - see §3
- Cargo insurance - see §4
- DGR / lithium-ion handling - +USD 0.45/kg, min USD 180/shipment
- After-hours pickup or delivery - +20% on origin/destination handling
- DDP-mode duty / VAT pass-through (charged at landed cost + 1.5% admin)

## 4. Cargo insurance (optional but recommended)

- Standard K+N InsureCargo: 0.18% of declared CIF value, min USD 35/shipment
- High-value goods (declared > USD 250,000): bespoke quote, contact K+N InsureCargo team
- All-risk cover incl. theft, water damage, transit delay > 72 h
- Not included in base rate - must be elected at booking

## 8. Required customer information before final quote can be issued

To convert an indicative quote into a firm rate agreement, K+N needs:

1. **HS codes** for the goods (mixed consumer electronics could span 8517 / 8528 / 8543 etc.)
2. **DGR status** — written confirmation no lithium-ion batteries > 100 Wh, or PI 965/966/967 detail
3. **Average and max single-shipment value** (for insurance and customs guarantee)
4. **INCO terms preference** (DAP recommended; DDP needs duty deposit setup)
5. **DXB receiver** (FZ name, license number, importer of record)
6. **Pickup address Singapore** and any access constraints (truck height, loading bay times)
7. **Volume confidence** — firm vs. forecast for the initial 12 weeks
8. **Peak season planning** — if commitment extends into Aug-Oct, advise on volume tolerance

Per 12-week program: **~USD 91,400 - 93,150** depending on insurance elected and weekly tonnage variation.
