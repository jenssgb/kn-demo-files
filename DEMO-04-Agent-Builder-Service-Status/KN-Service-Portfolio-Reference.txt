# Kuehne+Nagel Service Portfolio - Quick Reference

> Internal reference document for account briefings. Use this to quickly map a customer's needs to KN service offerings.

## Sea Logistics

- **Full Container Load (FCL)** - Standard 20'/40' container shipments on all major trade lanes.
- **Less than Container Load (LCL)** - Consolidated shipments for smaller volumes; weekly direct services on top lanes.
- **Reefer / Temperature Controlled** - Pharma, food, perishables. Continuous temperature monitoring.
- **Project & Out-of-Gauge** - Heavy lift, breakbulk, oversize cargo.
- **seaexplorer** - KN's market intelligence and schedule reliability platform for ocean freight. Carrier and port performance benchmarking.

## Air Logistics

- **Standard Air Freight** - Door-to-door and airport-to-airport, all major hubs.
- **KN Express** - Time-definite express service for urgent shipments.
- **KN PharmaChain** - GDP-compliant air freight for pharmaceutical and healthcare.
- **KN SecureChain** - Aerospace and high-value cargo with chain-of-custody guarantees.
- **Charter** - Full and partial charter solutions.

## Road Logistics

- **European Groupage Network** - Daily linehauls across Europe (LTL/FTL).
- **Direct Loads (FTL)** - Dedicated trucks, time-definite.
- **Intermodal** - Combined road/rail for sustainability-focused customers.

## Contract Logistics

- **Warehousing** - Multi-user and dedicated facilities globally.
- **Value-Added Services** - Pick & pack, kitting, labeling, returns processing.
- **E-Commerce Fulfillment** - B2C order processing and last-mile coordination.
- **Industry verticals** - Automotive, aerospace, pharma, retail/FMCG, industrial.

## Customs & Trade Compliance

- **Customs Brokerage** - Import/export clearance in all major markets.
- **Trade Advisory** - Tariff classification, origin rules, free trade agreements.
- **Compliance Programs** - AEO, C-TPAT, sanctioned party screening.

## Digital Platforms

- **myKN** - Customer portal for quoting, booking, tracking across all modes.
- **seaexplorer** - Sea freight market intelligence.
- **KN ESI (Estimated Time of Arrival)** - Predictive ETA based on AI and live carrier data.
- **Visibility & Control Towers** - End-to-end shipment visibility, exception management.

## Sustainability

- **Net Zero Carbon program** - Carbon insetting via Sustainable Aviation Fuel (SAF), biofuel for ocean, and biodiesel for road.
- **CO2 reporting** - Per shipment carbon footprint via myKN.
- **Book & Claim** - Verified carbon reduction certificates.

## Typical Account Conversation Topics

- Service coverage and reliability on a specific trade lane.
- Switching modes (sea-to-air, air-to-sea) for cost or speed optimization.
- Vertical-specific compliance (pharma GDP, aerospace AS9120, automotive).
- Digital integration (EDI, API, myKN onboarding).
- Sustainability targets and carbon reduction options.
- Disruption response and contingency planning.
