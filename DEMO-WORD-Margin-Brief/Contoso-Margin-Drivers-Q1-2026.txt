# Contoso Industries — Q1 2026 Margin Drivers

> **Owner:** Petra Vogel, Pricing Analyst · **Status:** internal working note ·
> **Last updated:** 2026-05-18
> **Source dataset:** `KN-Q1-2026-Shipments.xlsx` (KN Margin Analyzer)

## Headline

K+N margin on Contoso Industries book of business is **-3.8 pp** vs. Q4 2025
baseline. Contoso ranks **#1 worst-margin customer** in K+N's Q1 2026 dataset.
Three identifiable drivers explain ~82 % of the gap; the residual ~18 % is
mix effects and FX.

## Driver 1 — Hamburg port demurrage on reefer FCL

- **Trade lane:** DE Hamburg → SG Singapore, reefer FCL
- **Impact:** ~ **-1.6 pp** of the Q1 margin gap
- **Root cause:** 14 reefer containers held at Hamburg port for 4–9 days due
  to documentation mismatch on the new MRN-format customs declaration
  (introduced 2026-01-15)
- **Financial exposure:** EUR 38,400 demurrage (CASE-2026-0142, in dispute
  with port authority)
- **Operational status:** documentation template fixed at K+N side
  (2026-02-12); no further excursions since
- **Recommendation:** push port authority for goodwill split on the disputed
  amount; close case by end May 2026

## Driver 2 — Frankfurt cold-chain temperature excursion

- **Service:** Pharma cold-chain Road Logistics
- **Impact:** ~ **-1.4 pp** of the Q1 margin gap
- **Root cause:** cross-dock chiller unit failed during a 6-hour transfer
  window for batch CTI-X48; 3 pallets recorded above 8 °C for 90 minutes
- **Financial exposure:** product write-off claim from Contoso under review
  (notional ~EUR 110k product value, K+N liability cap EUR 25k per shipment
  per contract)
- **Operational status:** chiller replaced; cross-dock spot-check protocol
  upgraded (2026-04-08)
- **Recommendation:** prepare proactive customer-side narrative — incident
  contained, controls strengthened, no second excursion in 4 weeks since

## Driver 3 — APAC user provisioning lag → manual handling

- **Service:** APAC inbound Sea + Air, all flows
- **Impact:** ~ **-0.7 pp** of the Q1 margin gap
- **Root cause:** Contoso's Singapore and Shanghai operations users could
  not authenticate to the KN portal for ~5 weeks (2026-01-08 → 2026-02-14)
  due to a M365 licensing snag on their side; K+N had to run their
  shipments via manual exception processing
- **Financial exposure:** ~EUR 22k of additional handling time absorbed by
  K+N
- **Operational status:** resolved at user level; portal access restored
- **Recommendation:** post-mortem with Hiroshi Tanaka — make this a joint
  process improvement instead of a finger-pointing exercise

## Residual ~0.1 pp

- Minor FX exposure on USD-denominated APAC lane
- Volume mix shifts within the FCL portfolio

## Q2 2026 trajectory

If the three drivers above are contained (high confidence on #1 and #2,
medium on #3 staying clean), margin should recover by **~2.5 pp** in Q2.
Full recovery to Q4 2025 baseline expected by Q3 2026 if Wrocław plant
ramps as announced and shifts ~120 TEU out of APAC Sea inbound (lower-margin
mode replaced by Road Logistics inside the EU).
